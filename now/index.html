Now

A snapshot of what I'm up to right now.

Building

  • This website. A full redesign that started as "tidy a few things up" and became something more substantial. New pages, shared CSS architecture, a proper blog API with SQLite, and a content overhaul. The fact you're reading this means it shipped.
  • Homelab improvements. Better visibility into what's actually running on my Synology. Adding proper monitoring and alerting so I know about problems before the site goes dark and someone has to tell me.
  • Writing more. Publishing has always been the part I put off. I'm trying to lower the bar. Shorter posts are fine; not everything needs to be exhaustive.

At work

Software engineering at IBM, working within the CICS product. Right now the focus is on test automation: building frameworks and tooling that let the team move faster while catching regressions before they reach customers. A lot of the interesting work is in the infrastructure around testing rather than the tests themselves: making failure diagnosis fast, keeping environments reproducible, reducing the friction between "write a test" and "see results."

Also watching the AI tooling space closely, both as a user (these things are genuinely useful when applied well) and as an engineer trying to understand where they fit into serious software development rather than just demos.

Learning

  • Go. Slowly working through the language. Not because I have an immediate need, but because it's good at the kind of things I care about (fast, simple, statically typed, excellent tooling) and I'd rather learn it before I need it than during.
  • Infrastructure as code. Moving away from "I'll remember what I did to set this up" toward actually codifying my homelab configuration. Ansible for now, possibly Terraform later.
  • Writing clearly. Treating this as a technical skill, not a personality trait. Editing my own work more aggressively. The first draft is always worse than I think.

Outside of work

  • Football. Season is in full swing. Results have been a reliable source of strong feelings. I follow the tactical patterns as much as the scoreline. It's more interesting that way and the disappointment is the same either way.
  • Aviation. Currently reading about the development history of the A380: the economics, the engineering decisions, the way it became simultaneously a marvel and a commercial miscalculation. Fascinating case study.
  • Gym. Go most days. Good for switching off from work and the one thing that has a fixed slot in the day regardless of what else is going on.
  • Film. Working through a backlog. Recent highlights: rewatching Blade Runner 2049, which holds up better than most sequels have any right to.
